# BTC-USDT $10,000 compounding simulation: 2022-08-13 to 2025-10-07

## Result: profitable, but not a good multi-year return

The corrected, compounding 25-level grid turned **10,000 USDT into
10,556.126729–10,570.503421 USDT**, a total return of **5.5613%–5.7050%** over
1,152 days. That is approximately **1.73%–1.77% annualized**. The range comes from running both
possible one-minute OHLC paths because candles do not reveal whether each minute's high or low
occurred first.

BTC-USDT rose from 24,400.0 to 121,344.9 USDT, or **397.3152%**. A fee-adjusted BTC buy-and-hold
benchmark ended at **49,681.834559 USDT**. The grid ended entirely in USDT and captured very little
of the rally.

Compounding added only **11.43–12.11 USDT** relative to the corrected fixed-size result. Fees
consumed about **47.1% of gross grid-cycle profit**. Equity also fell roughly 22.7% below the
starting balance at its minimum, so the return was weak relative to the modeled risk.

## Why compounding did not produce a large return

After each sell, the simulator resized the next buy to `current equity × 65% ÷ 25`, and the paired
sell used the exact BTC quantity purchased. This worked: the initial 260 USDT order size eventually
reached approximately 275 USDT.

However, compounding can only affect orders that continue filling. The fixed grid remained centered
near the 24,400 USDT starting price. Its final fill occurred on 2023-09-12; it then placed no more
fills for the remainder of the test while BTC climbed far above the grid. An executable upward
recenter policy is therefore more important than compounding for this historical path.

## Chunked data acquisition and validation

The public OKX historical-candles API was downloaded into **39 restartable monthly/partial-month
chunks**. Each chunk was written atomically and accepted only when its first timestamp, final
timestamp, row count, and one-minute continuity matched its requested boundaries. The validated
chunks were then aggregated locally.

- Requested inclusive period: 2022-08-13 00:00 through 2025-10-07 23:59 UTC
- Daily candles: 1,152
- Minute candles: 1,658,880 (`1,152 × 1,440`)
- Missing intervals: 0
- Duplicate timestamps: 0
- Invalid OHLC rows: 0
- Unconfirmed candles: 0
- Chunk directory: `data/chunks/20220813_to_20251007/` (local and gitignored)
- Aggregated minute file: `data/okx_btc_usdt_1m_20220813_to_20251007.csv` (local and gitignored)
- Daily file: `data/okx_btc_usdt_1d_20220813_to_20251007.csv`
- Machine-readable results: `../results/2022-08-13_to_2025-10-07_v1.json`

## Assumptions and limitations

- Starting balance: 10,000 USDT, including the BTC bootstrap needed for the initial sell ladder
- Account fee assumption: maker 0.08%, taker 0.10%
- Effective grid spacing: 0.32%
- Active allocation: 65%; reserve: 35%; 25 levels (12 buy, 13 sell)
- Every touched limit order is optimistically assumed to fill completely as maker
- Queue position, spread, latency, slippage, partial fills, post-only rejects, outages, and tax are
  excluded
- The current account fee tier is applied historically because historical account-specific fee
  tiers were unavailable
- Recenter and reserve-deployment behavior are absent because the repository does not define them
  as executable algorithms

This remains a strategy proxy. The application itself is still read-only and cannot execute,
compound, reconcile, or recover real orders.
