# BTC-USDT 2024 $100 grid simulation

## Result

Using the same corrected 25-level strategy proxy and assumptions as the 2026 audit, a 100 USDT
starting balance on 2024-01-01 finished 2024 at **103.818981–103.892130 USDT**, a return of
**3.8190%–3.8921%**. The range represents the two possible intraminute OHLC paths because candles
do not reveal whether each minute's high or low occurred first.

BTC-USDT rose from 42,284.0 to 93,585.1 USDT during the period, a **121.3251%** increase. A
fee-adjusted BTC buy-and-hold benchmark ended at **221.103984 USDT**. The grid was profitable but
captured little of the trend because it sold BTC into the rally and has no upward-recenter rule.
Both simulated paths ended entirely in USDT after the initial grid's BTC inventory was sold.

## Inputs and validation

- Period: 2024-01-01 00:00 through 2024-12-31 23:59 UTC
- Starting capital: 100 USDT
- Initial BTC bootstrap: enough BTC to fund the 13 opening sell levels
- Active allocation: 65%; reserve: 35%; 25 levels (12 buy, 13 sell)
- Account fee assumption: maker 0.08%, taker 0.10%
- Effective spacing: 0.32%, derived by the repository's fee-aware rule
- Compounding: after each sell, the next buy is resized to `current equity × 65% ÷ 25`; its paired
  sell uses the exact BTC quantity acquired
- Tick size: 0.1 USDT; lot size: 0.00000001 BTC; minimum size: 0.00001 BTC

The OKX public historical-candles API returned exactly **366 daily candles** and **527,040 minute
candles**, matching every minute of leap year 2024. Both datasets have zero missing intervals,
duplicate timestamps, invalid OHLC rows, or unconfirmed candles.

Artifacts:

- `data/okx_btc_usdt_1d_2024.csv`: requested daily extract
- `data/okx_btc_usdt_1m_2024.csv`: local backtest input (gitignored because of its size)
- `../results/2024.json`: complete machine-readable output
- `../grid_backtest.py`: parameterized downloader and simulator

## Interpretation and caveats

The comparable fixed-size result was 103.762016–103.832502 USDT, so compounding added roughly
0.057–0.060 USDT in this window. The improvement from the declining 2026 result is real within
this model, but it does not establish
that the strategy is generally profitable. The test covers one known historical path, with no
walk-forward parameter selection or out-of-sample evaluation. The current account fee tier is
assumed for all of 2024 because historical account-specific fee tiers were unavailable.

The model optimistically treats every touched limit price as a complete maker fill. It excludes
queue position, spread, latency, slippage, partial fills, post-only rejects, outages, and tax. It
also cannot simulate upward/downward recentering or reserve deployment because those policies have
not yet been implemented as executable rules. Consequently, this is a strategy proxy rather than
evidence that the current read-only application is safe to trade.
